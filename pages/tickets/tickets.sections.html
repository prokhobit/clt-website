Questions

Ticket FAQ

How do reserved seats work?

Every ticket is for a specific seat. Choose a performance, pick your seats on the map, and they are held for you while you check out. Rows A–C are Premium ($65), D–F Standard ($55) and H–I Value ($45).

How do I get my tickets?

Your e-tickets arrive by email as soon as you check out. Show them on your phone at the door, or print them.

How long is the show, and is it right for my child?

Pinocchio: The Musical runs about 60 minutes and is recommended for ages 4 and up.

Can I change or refund my tickets?

Write to us through the contact form with your order details and we will help.
